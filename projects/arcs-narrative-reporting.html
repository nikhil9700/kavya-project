Case study 06 Portfolio knowledge project

ARCS Automation + Narrative Reporting Pack

A close-quality design that pairs ARCS reconciliation workflow and risk-based inventory with a Narrative Reporting package — so balances are supported and the story is leadership-ready.

Knowledge / portfolio case study only. Not employment history or named client work.

Read full case study Contact Kavya
Objective ARCS design Automation Narrative pack Controls Modules Deliverables Outcomes

Objective

Improve close confidence by designing a reconciliation operating model in ARCS and a Narrative Reporting package that turns reconciled results into clear commentary for leadership review.

ARCS reconciliation design

  • Reconciliation inventory classified by risk and materiality
  • Profile attributes, frequencies, and evidence expectations
  • Preparer / reviewer workflow and due-date model
  • Aging and escalation for past-due reconciliations
  • Link between ARCS completion status and close calendar gates
Risk-based effort
High-risk accounts get tighter frequency and stronger evidence; low-risk accounts avoid busywork.
Evidence standards
Each profile type defines what “done” means — not just a checkbox.

Automation & matching opportunities

  • Identify accounts suitable for auto-certification or transaction matching concepts
  • Define safe boundaries where automation is allowed vs. always-manual review
  • Document exception queues created by failed matches
  • Measure success as reduced manual effort without reduced control quality

Narrative Reporting pack

  1. 01
    Package structure Fixed section order for monthly / quarterly close storytelling.
  2. 02
    Owners Who drafts, who reviews, who approves each section.
  3. 03
    Commentary standards Variance, risk, and close-status language templates.
  4. 04
    Source link ARCS / close completion signals what leadership can trust.
  5. 05
    Distribution Timing aligned to consolidation and reporting gates.

Suggested narrative sections

  • Close status & open risks
  • P&L highlights and major variances
  • Balance sheet / working capital notes
  • Intercompany / consolidation commentary
  • Reconciliation health summary from ARCS
  • Actions and owners for next period

Controls & risks

  • No narrative pack finalization while critical reconciliations remain past due
  • Reviewer cannot be the same as preparer on high-risk profiles
  • Risk: automation hiding real breaks — mitigated by exception review queues
  • Risk: narrative becoming slide fluff — mitigated by required source references

Deliverables

Outcomes

  • A reconciliation design that shows control thinking beyond transaction processing
  • A narrative pack outline suitable for leadership-style review discussions
  • A complete portfolio arc from Fusion foundation through EPM close storytelling
